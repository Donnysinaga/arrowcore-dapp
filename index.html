Frequently Asked Questions

How does Arrow Core hide my transactions?
We use a combination of zk-SNARKs and shielded mempools. When you deposit funds, they are pooled with others, and when you withdraw, cryptographic proofs ensure you own the funds without linking the withdrawal address to the deposit address.
Is it compatible with MetaMask?
Yes, Arrow Core acts as an intermediary layer. You can connect standard Web3 wallets like MetaMask, TrustWallet, or Rabby, and execute shielded transactions directly from our dashboard.
What are the fees?
Arrow Core charges a flat 0.1% protocol fee for mixing and routing, plus the standard network gas fee of the underlying blockchain (e.g., Ethereum or Polygon).
