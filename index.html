How it works

Deposit → Commit → Prove → Settle.

01

Deposit

Funds move into the Arrow Core vault contract on Robinhood Chain. A cryptographic commitment is generated in your browser.

02

Commit

Your commitment is inserted into an on-chain Merkle tree. The new root is published, but the leaf content stays private.

03

Prove

To withdraw, your browser generates a Zero-Knowledge proof of tree membership — without revealing which leaf you own.

04

Settle

The signed ZK proof is relayed on-chain. The contract verifies and releases funds to your chosen address. No link to origin.

FAQ

Questions worth answering upfront.

How does Arrow Core protect my privacy?
Arrow Core uses zero-knowledge Merkle proofs. When you deposit, a cryptographic commitment is created. When you withdraw, a ZK proof shows you own a leaf in the tree without revealing which one — breaking any on-chain link between your deposit and withdrawal address.
Who pays the withdrawal gas?
Arrow Core runs a subsidized relayer network on Robinhood Chain. The relayer submits your withdrawal transaction on-chain and pays the gas fee, so you can withdraw to a completely fresh wallet with zero prior ETH balance.
Is this legal to use?
Arrow Core is a privacy infrastructure protocol, not a mixer for illicit funds. Privacy is a fundamental right. Users are responsible for complying with the laws of their jurisdiction. The protocol enforces on-chain compliance tooling compatible with regulatory frameworks.
How do AI agents interact with my shielded funds?
Agents connect via an encrypted MCP (Model Context Protocol) server. They receive your wallet context in a sandboxed, encrypted session — they can read balances and trigger transactions, but they never have access to raw private keys or plaintext credentials.
What chain is Arrow Core deployed on?
Arrow Core is deployed natively on Robinhood Chain (Chain ID: 4663). Robinhood Chain is a zero-fee, retail-focused EVM chain built for mass adoption with instant finality.
