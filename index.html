03 How it works

Deposit → Commit → Prove → Settle.

A four-step flow that guarantees mathematical unlinkability between your deposit and withdrawal addresses — verified on-chain, no trusted setup.

01 ─ DEPOSIT
01

Funds enter the vault

ETH moves into the Arrow Core vault contract on Robinhood Chain. A cryptographic commitment is generated entirely in your browser — your keys never leave.

02 ─ COMMIT
02

Commitment joins the tree

Your commitment is inserted as a leaf into an on-chain Merkle tree. The new root is published, but the leaf content — linking you to the deposit — stays hidden.

03 ─ PROVE
03

ZK proof generated

To withdraw, your browser computes a zero-knowledge proof that you own a leaf in the tree — without revealing which leaf, nullifier, or deposit amount.

04 ─ SETTLE
04

On-chain settlement

The ZK proof goes to a relayer, which submits the withdrawal and pays gas. Funds arrive at any address you choose. No trace back to origin.

05 FAQ

Questions worth answering upfront.

How does Arrow Core protect my privacy? +
Arrow Core uses zero-knowledge Merkle proofs (zk-SNARKs). When you deposit, a cryptographic commitment is created entirely in your browser. When you withdraw, a ZK proof shows you own a valid commitment in the Merkle tree without revealing which one — permanently breaking the on-chain link between your deposit and withdrawal addresses.
Who pays my withdrawal gas fees? +
Arrow Core runs a subsidized relayer network on Robinhood Chain (a zero-fee chain). The relayer submits your withdrawal transaction on your behalf, so you can receive funds into a completely fresh wallet with zero prior ETH balance. You pay no gas.
How do AI agents use my shielded portfolio? +
Agents connect via an encrypted MCP (Model Context Protocol) server. They receive a sandboxed, ciphertext-only view of your portfolio — enough to execute trades and rebalancing strategies, but never enough to access your raw private keys or unencrypted credentials. All AI inference is routed via Tor.
Is using Arrow Core legal? +
Arrow Core is financial privacy infrastructure, not a tool for illicit activity. Privacy is a fundamental right. The protocol is built with on-chain compliance tooling compatible with evolving regulatory frameworks. Users are responsible for complying with local laws.
What chain is Arrow Core on? +
Arrow Core is deployed natively on Robinhood Chain (Chain ID: 4663) — a retail-focused, zero-fee EVM chain built for mass adoption with instant finality and a developer-friendly environment.
